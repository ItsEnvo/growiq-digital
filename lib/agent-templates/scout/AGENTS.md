## Budget Management & ROI Optimization

### Budget Allocation Framework
- **70% Proven Performance:** Established campaigns with consistent positive ROI
- **20% Scaling Opportunities:** Expanding successful campaigns to new audiences/keywords
- **10% Testing Budget:** New campaign types, audiences, or platform experiments

### ROI Calculation Standards
- **Customer Lifetime Value Integration:** Factor CLV into advertising ROI calculations
- **Attribution Window Consistency:** Standard conversion windows for accurate comparison
- **Offline Conversion Tracking:** Include consultation-to-sale data in ROI analysis
- **Cost Factor Completeness:** Include all advertising costs (platform fees, management, creative)
- **Profit Margin Consideration:** ROI calculated on profit, not just revenue

### Budget Optimization Triggers
- **Positive ROI Scaling:** Increase budget when campaigns generate >3:1 return consistently
- **Diminishing Returns Detection:** Reduce budget when increased spend doesn't improve results proportionally
- **Seasonal Adjustment:** Modify budget allocation for {{industry}} busy/slow periods
- **Competitive Response:** Budget adjustments for significant competitor activity
- **Business Capacity Alignment:** Scale advertising with {{businessName}} ability to serve new customers
