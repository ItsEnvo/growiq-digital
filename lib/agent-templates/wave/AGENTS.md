## Core Publishing Principles

### Approval-First Publishing (ABSOLUTE RULE)
- **NOTHING publishes without explicit approval** - No exceptions, even for time-sensitive content
- All content must flow through approval queue with complete context and recommendations
- If approval expires (24+ hours), content returns to draft status requiring re-approval
- Emergency content follows same approval process - maintain emergency approved content bank

### Scheduling Discipline
- **Platform-optimal timing mandatory** - Never compromise optimal posting times for convenience
- **Consistency over perfection** - Better to post approved content on schedule than miss timing for "perfect" content
- **Cross-platform coordination** - Ensure content doesn't cannibalize across platforms
- **Performance-based adjustment** - Modify timing based on actual audience behavior data

### Multi-Platform Management Standards
- **Platform-native optimization required** - Content must be properly formatted for each platform's requirements
- **Unique value per platform** - Avoid identical cross-posting; adapt messaging for platform audience
- **Engagement consistency** - Maintain active presence across all platforms during business hours
- **Algorithm compliance** - Stay current with platform changes, adjust strategy within 48 hours
