## Algorithm Deep Dive by Platform

### Instagram Algorithm (2024 Updates)

**Ranking Factors in Order of Importance:**
1. **Interest Prediction:** Based on user's past behavior with similar content
2. **Relationship Score:** How often user interacts with account
3. **Timeliness:** How recent the post is (weighted heavily in first 6 hours)
4. **Frequency:** How often user opens Instagram affects fresh content priority
5. **Following:** Total accounts followed affects individual post visibility
6. **Usage:** Total time spent on app affects depth of feed shown

**Algorithm Optimization Strategies:**
- **Golden Hour Publishing:** First 60 minutes determine long-term reach
- **Engagement Velocity:** Quick likes/comments exponentially boost distribution
- **Save Rate Priority:** Instagram heavily weighs content worth revisiting
- **Share Rate Multiplier:** DM shares create massive reach expansion beyond followers
- **Story Engagement:** Regular story viewers see feed posts more frequently
- **Feature Adoption:** New features get algorithm boost for 2-4 weeks

**Content Type Performance Ranking:**
1. **Reels:** Highest reach potential, especially with trending audio
2. **Carousel Posts:** Strong engagement, longer viewing time
3. **Single Image Posts:** Consistent performance, good for brand building
4. **Video Posts:** Declining favor compared to Reels
5. **IGTV:** Lower priority unless exceptional engagement

### TikTok For You Page Algorithm

**Primary Ranking Signals:**
1. **Video Completion Rate:** Most critical metric—users watching entire video
2. **Re-watch Rate:** Users watching video multiple times
3. **Share Rate:** Especially external shares (text, email, other platforms)
4. **Comment Rate:** Meaningful comments weighted over emoji reactions
5. **Like Rate:** Still important but lower weighted than above metrics
6. **Profile Visit Rate:** Indicates strong content interest

**TikTok-Specific Optimization:**
- **3-Second Rule:** Algorithm judges engagement potential in first 3 seconds
- **Native Content Priority:** Phone-shot videos outperform professionally produced
- **Sound Strategy:** Trending sounds boost discovery, original sounds build brand
- **Hashtag Strategy:** 3-5 hashtags maximum, mix trending + niche + branded
- **Engagement Speed:** Comments in first hour heavily weighted
- **Geographic Relevance:** Local content performs better in local feeds

**Content Categories Performance:**
1. **Educational/How-To:** Highest save and share rates
2. **Entertainment/Humor:** Highest engagement velocity
3. **Transformation/Before-After:** Strong completion rates
4. **Trending Participation:** Temporary boost, requires quick execution
5. **Behind-the-Scenes:** Authentic feel drives higher engagement

### LinkedIn Professional Algorithm

**Business Feed Ranking Factors:**
1. **Dwell Time:** Time spent reading/viewing content
2. **Engagement Quality:** Comments weighted more than likes
3. **Professional Relevance:** Industry-related content prioritized
4. **Network Relationship:** 1st connections heavily favored
5. **Content Recency:** Business hours posting significantly advantaged
6. **Creator Authority:** Established professional profiles get reach boost

**LinkedIn-Specific Strategies:**
- **Text-Heavy Content:** 1,300-2,000 characters perform best
- **Native Video Priority:** Uploaded directly vs shared from other platforms
- **Professional Storytelling:** Challenge + journey + insight + application
- **Industry Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant industry tags, avoid generic hashtags
- **Comment Conversations:** Meaningful dialogue boosts all participants' reach
- **Document Posts:** Native multi-slide feature gets algorithm preference

### X/Twitter Timeline Algorithm

**Tweet Distribution Logic:**
1. **Recency:** Real-time nature means timing is most critical
2. **Engagement Velocity:** Likes, retweets, comments in first 15 minutes
3. **Relationship Score:** Users who regularly interact with account
4. **Interest Matching:** Based on user's engagement history
5. **Conversation Quality:** Meaningful replies boost thread visibility
6. **External Click Rate:** Though may reduce organic reach

**Twitter Optimization Tactics:**
- **Peak Hours Critical:** Tweet during audience's active browsing times
- **Thread Strategy:** Connected tweets get bundled visibility
- **Quote Tweet Value:** Adding commentary to trending topics increases reach
- **Hashtag Restraint:** 1-2 hashtags maximum, avoid hashtag stuffing
- **Media Integration:** Images/videos get slight algorithm preference
- **Reply Engagement:** Quick responses to comments boost thread performance

### Facebook Community Algorithm

**News Feed Distribution Factors:**
1. **Meaningful Social Interactions:** Comments prioritized over likes
2. **Content Type Preference:** Native video > photos > links > text
3. **Time Decay:** Severe penalty for aged content compared to other platforms
4. **Relationship Score:** Friends/followed pages interaction history
5. **Group Participation:** Group content often outperforms page content
6. **Local Relevance:** Geographic proximity weighted for local businesses

**Facebook Strategy Adaptation:**
- **Video-First Approach:** Native video uploads get significant reach boost
- **Comment-Driving Content:** Ask questions, create discussion prompts
- **Local Community Focus:** Geographic and community relevance matters most
- **Group Cross-Posting:** Tailor content for community groups, not broadcast
- **Link Strategy:** Place links in comments to maintain organic reach
- **Event Integration:** Facebook Events get special algorithm treatment

## Crisis Communication & Reputation Management

### Crisis Identification Matrix

**Level 1 - Standard Customer Service:**
- Individual customer complaint about service experience
- Booking confusion or miscommunication
- General questions about services or policies
- **Response:** Professional customer service, move to private messages
- **Timeline:** Within 2 hours during business hours

**Level 2 - Reputation Management:**
- Multiple similar complaints indicating systemic issue
- Negative review requiring public response
- Misinformation about services or industry
- **Response:** Public acknowledgment, professional correction, private follow-up
- **Timeline:** Within 1 hour, escalate to management

**Level 3 - Crisis Management:**
- Legal issues, safety concerns, or regulatory violations
- Viral negative content or major PR issue
- Industry-wide controversy affecting business
- **Response:** Immediate management escalation, potential posting pause
- **Timeline:** Within 30 minutes, follow crisis communication plan

### Public Response Templates (Customizable by {{brandTone}})

**Negative Review Response Framework:**
1. **Acknowledge:** "Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback..."
2. **Apologize (if appropriate):** "We're sorry to hear about your experience..."
3. **Action:** "We'd like to make this right. Please send us a DM so we can..."
4. **Improvement:** "We use feedback like this to improve our service for everyone."
5. **Invitation:** "We hope you'll give us another opportunity to serve you better."

**Misinformation Correction Template:**
1. **Professional Tone:** "Thanks for bringing this to our attention..."
2. **Factual Correction:** "Here's the accurate information about [topic]..."
3. **Source Authority:** "According to [credible source/certification/experience]..."
4. **Educational Value:** "We hope this clarifies [topic] for everyone."
5. **Open Communication:** "Feel free to DM us with any other questions."
