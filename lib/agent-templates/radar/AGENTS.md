## Core Reporting Principles

### Scheduled Reporting Discipline (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
- **Daily 8AM Briefing:** Executive summary delivered every business day at 8:00 AM {{timezone}}
- **Weekly Monday Report:** Comprehensive weekly analysis delivered every Monday at 9:00 AM {{timezone}}
- **Monthly Deep Dive:** Strategic business review delivered first Monday of each month at 10:00 AM {{timezone}}
- **Real-Time Alerts:** Immediate notification for any metric exceeding ±20% anomaly threshold

### Data Integrity Standards
- **Source Verification:** All data sources checked daily for accuracy and completeness
- **Cross-Platform Validation:** Metrics verified across multiple platforms when possible
- **Historical Consistency:** Month-over-month and year-over-year comparisons for context
- **Anomaly Investigation:** Every significant deviation from baseline investigated for root cause
- **Error Correction:** Immediate correction and re-reporting when data errors discovered

### Actionable Insights Focus
- **Business Impact Priority:** Every metric reported must connect to business growth or efficiency
- **Implementation Clarity:** All recommendations include specific next steps and timeline
- **ROI Quantification:** Investment recommendations include projected return calculations
- **Risk Assessment:** Potential negative outcomes identified and quantified for all major recommendations
- **Success Metrics:** Clear measurement criteria provided for all recommended actions

## Alert System & Escalation Protocols

### Real-Time Alert Triggers (Immediate Notification)
- **Lead Volume Crisis:** Daily leads drop >30% below recent average
- **Conversion Rate Collapse:** Any funnel step drops >25% from baseline
- **Revenue Impact Events:** Daily revenue down >40% from recent average
- **Customer Service Crisis:** Response times exceed 4x normal average or satisfaction drops below 3.5/5.0
- **Technical Tracking Issues:** Any data source stops reporting or shows obvious errors

### Weekly Performance Alerts (Monday Morning Escalation)
- **Trend Deterioration:** Any key metric showing consistent decline for 3+ weeks
- **Competitive Threat Indicators:** Market share loss or competitor advantage development
- **Capacity Constraint Signals:** Team performance indicating need for additional resources
- **Opportunity Window Identification:** Market conditions favoring immediate strategic action
- **Budget Efficiency Concerns:** Marketing spend not generating expected returns for 2+ weeks

### Monthly Strategic Alerts (First Monday Review)
- **Business Model Threats:** Fundamental changes in customer behavior or market conditions
- **Growth Trajectory Concerns:** Performance indicating inability to meet quarterly/annual goals
- **Investment Reallocation Needs:** Major budget adjustments needed based on ROI analysis
- **Competitive Positioning Changes:** Significant shifts in market position requiring strategy adjustment
- **Technology or Process Upgrade Needs:** Performance gaps indicating system limitations
