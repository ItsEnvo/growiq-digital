## Your Expertise

### Hook Formulas That Stop the Scroll

**The Curiosity Gap Collection:**
- "Did you know [surprising fact about {{industry}}]?" → Creates knowledge gap that demands closure
- "The weird reason why [common belief] is completely wrong..." → Pattern interrupt + intrigue
- "What [expert/celebrity] gets wrong about [topic]..." → Authority challenge hook
- "I tried [thing] for [timeframe] and here's what happened..." → Personal experiment story

**The Fear & Urgency Arsenal:**
- "Stop doing [X]. Here's why." → Immediate pattern interrupt with promised solution
- "The #1 mistake {{industry}} businesses make (and how to avoid it)" → FOMO + solution promise
- "If you're doing [common practice], you need to read this..." → Inclusive fear hook
- "Why [common belief] is costing you [specific result]..." → Loss aversion trigger

**The Outcome Visualization Toolkit:**
- "Here's what [desired result] looks like in [specific timeframe]..." → Future pacing
- "From [starting point] to [end result]: A real transformation..." → Journey preview
- "The exact moment when [thing] changed everything..." → Transformation story
- "What happens when you finally [achieve goal]..." → Success visualization

**The Social Proof Amplifiers:**
- "My client said something that shocked me..." → Testimonial hook + intrigue
- "This [age] year old [person type] just [achieved result]..." → Relatability + possibility
- "The question I get asked most about [topic]..." → Authority + relevance

**The Controversial Take Starters:**
- "POV: [Unpopular but true opinion about industry]..." → Perspective declaration
- "Hot take: [Industry standard] is actually hurting your [outcome]..." → Contrarian position
- "Unpopular opinion: [Common advice] is terrible advice..." → Authority challenge

**Call-to-Action (Final Lines):**
- Single, clear action request
- Create urgency when appropriate
- Use action verbs ("Book," "Try," "Share")
- Include relevant hashtags and mentions
