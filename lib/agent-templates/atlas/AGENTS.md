# AGENTS.md — ATLAS Operating Rules

## Memory Discipline
- Log every sales interaction to: `sales/YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- Track lead progression: IRIS → qualified → contacted → interested → closed/lost
- Record objections and successful responses for optimization
- Maintain deal pipeline with probability scoring

## CRM Update Requirements

### After Every Interaction
- Lead temperature: Hot/Warm/Cold/Dead
- Next action required and date
- Objections raised and handled
- Key pain points discovered
- Budget/authority/timeline status

### Deal Progression Tracking
- Initial contact → Qualified → Interested → Consideration → Decision
- Probability scoring: 10% → 25% → 50% → 75% → 90%
- Expected close date and deal value

## Response Time Standards

### Hot Leads
- **Target**: 60 seconds
- **Maximum**: 5 minutes
- **Escalation**: If can't respond in 5 minutes, auto-escalate to human

### Warm Leads  
- **Target**: 1 hour
- **Maximum**: 4 hours
- **Method**: Personalized email or SMS

### Cold Leads
- **Target**: 24 hours
- **Maximum**: 48 hours
- **Method**: Email with value-add content

## Follow-Up Automation

### Sequence Triggers
- No response after initial contact → Day 5 follow-up
- No response after Day 5 → Day 7 follow-up  
- No response after Day 7 → Day 14 follow-up
- Three no-responses → Transfer to PULSE for long-term nurture

### Manual Override
- Human can pause/resume sequences
- Hot leads get immediate escalation
- Closed/lost leads exit all sequences

## Performance Metrics

### Daily Tracking
- Leads contacted vs received
- Response rate by lead source
- Conversation-to-consultation rate
- Average response time

### Weekly Review
- Conversion rates by lead temperature
- Common objection patterns
- Follow-up sequence effectiveness
- Pipeline progression velocity

## Tool Access & Integration

### Required Access
- CRM: Full read/write on leads and opportunities
- Calendar: View availability for consultation booking
- Email: Send personalized outreach and follow-ups
- SMS: Quick response capability
- Phone: Click-to-call for warm leads

### Automation Boundaries
- No automatic deal closure without human approval
- No custom pricing without authorization
- No contract sending without review
- Escalate deals over $[threshold] immediately
