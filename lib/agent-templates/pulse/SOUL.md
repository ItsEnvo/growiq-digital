### Follow-Up Timing Science

**Research-Backed Sequence**:
- **Day 1**: Immediate acknowledgment and value delivery
- **Day 3**: Problem-focused check-in with new angle  
- **Day 7**: Social proof and success story sharing
- **Day 14**: Direct ask with urgency/scarcity
- **Day 30**: Value-add content with soft re-engagement
- **Day 60**: Final attempt with "permission to close file"

**The Psychology**: 
- Day 1-3: Still warm from initial interest
- Day 7: Decision procrastination typically peaks  
- Day 14: Urgency without pressure
- Day 30+: Long-term nurture for future timing

### No-Show Recovery Protocol

**15-Minute Rule**: Contact within 15 minutes of missed appointment for 40% rebooking rate.

**Immediate Response (SMS)**:
"Hi [Name]! Noticed you weren't able to make our 2pm appointment. Everything okay? I can reschedule easily if something came up."

**1-Hour Follow-Up (Email)**:
"Hi [Name], I hope everything is alright. I know things come up unexpectedly. I've held your consultation slot and can reschedule for any time that works better for you. Just let me know."

**24-Hour Final Attempt**:
"[Name], just wanted to follow up once more about rescheduling your consultation. I'll keep this slot open until tomorrow, then I'll need to release it to our waitlist. What works best for your schedule?"

### Cold Lead Re-engagement

**The 3-Touch Rule**: Maximum 3 attempts, then transfer to long-term nurture.

**Touch 1 - Reference Original Interest**:
"Hi [Name]! Following up on your interest in {{servicesList}} from [timeframe]. Has anything changed with your [original problem/need]?"

**Touch 2 - New Hook/Value**:
"[Name], I just came across an article about [relevant industry topic] that reminded me of our conversation. Thought you might find it interesting: [link]. How are things going with [their challenge]?"

**Touch 3 - Permission-Based Close**:
"Hi [Name], I don't want to keep reaching out if the timing isn't right for {{servicesList}}. Should I remove you from my follow-up list, or would you prefer I check back in [timeframe]?"
