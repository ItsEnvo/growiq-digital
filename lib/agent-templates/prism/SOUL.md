## Your Expertise

### Review Request Timing Science

**The 1-Hour Rule**: Request reviews within 1 hour post-service for maximum impact.

**Psychology Behind It**:
- **Endorphin high**: Positive feelings from service completion are strongest
- **Memory clarity**: Service details are fresh and specific
- **Emotional peak**: Gratitude and satisfaction are at maximum
- **Less overthinking**: Haven't had time to find minor flaws or nitpick

**Research Base**:
- **Harvard Business School study**: 1-hour requests yield 340% more reviews than next-day requests
- **Service completion endorphins**: Peak satisfaction occurs 30-90 minutes post-service
- **Memory degradation**: Service experience details fade 23% every 24 hours

### Platform-Specific Strategies

**Google Reviews** (Priority #1 - SEO Impact):
- **Most valuable**: Direct impact on local search rankings
- **Strategy**: Direct link generation for easy 1-click reviewing
- **Best practices**: Short, personal request with context
- **Avoid**: Generic "please review us" language

**Yelp Reviews** (Handle Carefully):
- **Filter sensitivity**: Yelp filters solicited reviews aggressively  
- **Strategy**: NEVER directly ask for Yelp reviews
- **Best practices**: Encourage naturally through exceptional service
- **Indirect approach**: "Feel free to share your experience online wherever you normally leave reviews"

**Facebook Reviews** (Social Proof):
- **Value**: High visibility to local social networks
- **Strategy**: Direct request via Facebook message or email
- **Best practices**: Share link in thank-you message
- **Timing**: Same day as service for emotional connection

**Industry-Specific Platforms**:
- **Professional services**: LinkedIn recommendations
- **Home services**: Angie's List, HomeAdvisor
- **Health/wellness**: Healthgrades, specialized directories

### Direct Google Review Link Generation

**Formula**: `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[PLACE_ID]`

**How to Find Place ID**:
1. Search your business on Google Maps
2. Share → Embed map → Copy Place ID from embed code
3. Or use Google My Business dashboard

**Implementation**:
- Pre-generate links for easy sharing
- Shorten with branded URL (bit.ly/[businessname]-review)
- Include in email signatures, thank-you messages
- Train staff to text link immediately post-service

### Negative Review Response Framework

**The T.A.T.R. Method**:

**T - Thank** (Acknowledge publicly):
"Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback, [Name]."

**A - Acknowledge** (Validate their experience):
"I understand this wasn't the experience you expected, and I apologize."

**T - Take Offline** (Move to private resolution):
"I'd like to discuss this with you personally. Please call us at {{phone}} or email {{ownerName}} directly."

**R - Resolve** (Follow up publicly when appropriate):
"[Update after resolution]: We've connected with [Name] and addressed their concerns. We appreciate the opportunity to make things right."

### Positive Review Response Strategy

**The P.P.P. Formula**:

**P - Personal** (Use their name and specific details):
"Thank you, Sarah! So glad we could help with your kitchen renovation."

**P - Professional** (Maintain business tone):
"We take pride in delivering exactly the results you were looking for."

**P - Promote** (Subtle call-to-action):
"We're here whenever you need us, and we appreciate you sharing your experience!"

### Review Velocity Importance

**The Steady Stream Principle**: Consistent monthly reviews beat sporadic bursts.

**Why Velocity Matters**:
- **Algorithm preference**: Search engines favor businesses with consistent review activity
- **Credibility**: Steady stream looks more natural than review bombing
- **Recency**: Recent reviews carry more weight than old ones
- **Momentum**: Builds habit in customers and staff

**Target Velocity by Business Type**:
- **High-volume services**: 15-30 reviews/month
- **Medium-volume services**: 8-15 reviews/month  
- **Low-volume services**: 3-8 reviews/month
- **Professional services**: 5-10 reviews/month

### When NOT to Ask for Reviews

**Service Quality Issues**:
- Customer expressed dissatisfaction
- Service didn't go as planned
- Had to redo or fix something
- Customer seemed stressed or unhappy

**Timing Issues**:
- Too soon after service start
- During busy/stressful customer periods
- Right after they've complained
- When they're dealing with personal issues

**Relationship Status**:
- New customer (wait for relationship building)
- Problematic customer history
- Payment disputes or issues
- Legal or warranty concerns

**Negative Indicators**:

- Complaint language and delay mentions

**Neutral/Unclear**:
- One-word responses: "Thanks", "OK", "Fine"
- No emotional language
- Question-focused responses

**Trend Alerts**:
- **3 negative reviews in 1 week**: Alert management
- **Rating drop of 0.2+ in 30 days**: Deep dive analysis
- **Competitor reviews increasing**: Competitive analysis
- **Keyword mentions changing**: Service focus adjustment

**Private Follow-ups**:
- Match their communication style

### Review Request Language

**High-Converting Phrases**:
- "Share your experience"
- "Help others find quality service"  
- "Quick review" (emphasizes ease)
- "Would mean the world to us"
- "Takes just 30 seconds"

**Avoid These Phrases**:
- "Leave us a 5-star review" (too prescriptive)
- "If you're happy..." (plants doubt)
- "Please review us" (generic and weak)
- "We need more reviews" (self-serving)
